Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

Welcome to Quilldrop: Mindset & Daily Quotes ("Quilldrop", "we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our mobile application.

1. About Quilldrop

Quilldrop is a motivational quotes application that provides quotes, themes, favorites, collections, personal quote libraries, widgets, reminders, and optional premium features.

You do not need to create an account to use the application. Quilldrop does not operate its own user account system or backend server for storing your personal quote library.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Quilldrop stores certain information locally on your device to provide its features. This may include:

  • Favorite quotes and collections.
  • Quotes and authors that you create.
  • Recently viewed quote history.
  • Selected themes, fonts, and app icon preferences.
  • Followed topics and categories.
  • Streak information and activity history.
  • Reminder and notification preferences.
  • Widget settings and customization preferences.

This information is stored using local device storage technologies, including Hive and SharedPreferences.

Quilldrop does not independently upload this locally stored information to its own servers. However, Android backup services may back up eligible application data to your Google account or transfer it to another device, depending on your device and backup settings.

3. Advertising

Quilldrop may display advertisements to users of the free version through Google AdMob. Premium subscribers do not receive advertisements while their premium access is active.

Google AdMob may collect or process information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers and device information.
  • IP address and approximate location information.
  • Advertising interactions and diagnostic information.
  • Information used for ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention.

Depending on your location, applicable laws, and your choices, Google may use information to provide personalized or non-personalized advertisements.

Quilldrop uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present privacy and consent options where required.

For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy.

4. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

Quilldrop offers optional premium features through in-app purchases, including monthly and yearly subscriptions and lifetime access.

Purchases are processed through Google Play. We use RevenueCat to manage subscription status, purchase validation, and premium access.

RevenueCat and Google Play may process information such as:

  • Purchase and transaction information.
  • Product and subscription details.
  • Purchase history and subscription status.
  • App user identifiers and purchase-related technical information.

Quilldrop does not directly collect or store your payment card information. Payment processing is handled by Google Play.

For more information:

  • RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • Google Payments

5. Notifications and Reminders

Quilldrop may schedule local notifications for quote reminders and streak reminders. These notifications are managed on your device and depend on the notification permissions and settings you provide.

You can manage notification permissions through your Android device settings.

6. Sharing and User-Generated Content

Quilldrop allows you to create personal quotes and share quotes using features provided by your device.

When you choose to share content, the selected content may be transferred to the third-party application or service you use. Their privacy policies and practices will apply.

Quilldrop does not independently publish your personal quotes or collections.

7. Analytics and Diagnostics

Quilldrop does not currently operate its own analytics or crash-reporting backend. However, third-party services and software development kits integrated into the application may process technical, diagnostic, advertising, or purchase-related information as described in this policy and their own policies.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored locally by Quilldrop generally remains on your device until you delete it, clear the application's data, or uninstall the application.

Eligible application data may also be included in Android backup and device-to-device transfer, depending on your device settings. As a result, some information may be restored when you set up Quilldrop on another device.

Information processed by Google AdMob, RevenueCat, and Google Play is subject to their respective retention and deletion practices.

You can manage or remove locally stored information by using available in-app options, clearing application data, or uninstalling Quilldrop.

9. Children's Privacy

Quilldrop is a general-audience application and is not specifically designed or marketed to children. We do not knowingly operate a service intended to collect personal information directly from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through a third-party service used by the application, please contact us so that we can review the concern.

10. Third-Party Services

Quilldrop relies on certain third-party services to provide advertising, purchase management, and platform functionality. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Relevant third-party services include:

  • Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform.
  • Google Play and Google Play Billing.
  • RevenueCat.
  • Android operating system backup and notification services.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of these services.

11. Your Choices

You can make choices about your information by:

  • Managing advertising and privacy choices where available.
  • Changing notification permissions in Android settings.
  • Managing or deleting locally stored application data.
  • Managing subscriptions through Google Play.
  • Adjusting Android backup settings on your device.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the application, its features, or applicable requirements. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
